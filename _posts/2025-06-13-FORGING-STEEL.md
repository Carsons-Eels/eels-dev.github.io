The STEEL team is beginning the exciting process of merging the EELS and EEST repositories. We believe this will be a positive move for our team, and for the project itself. By moving the initiative into a single code base, we will be able to standardize our workflows, processes, standards, tooling, and style in a way that will serve the core STEEL objective of producing a maximally comprehensible execution client and a robust set of consumable, standardized tests for all execution clients.
